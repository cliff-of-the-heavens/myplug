Get your images and files right the first time

The sizes, formats and file limits MyPlug checks when you upload a banner ad, a listing photo or a digital product, plus free tools to fix a file that doesn't fit.

1Banner ads

The rules the upload checks

  • Format: JPG, PNG or WebP. GIF, PDF and Word files are refused.
  • Shape: must match the spot to within about 8%. A larger image of the same shape, like 3200 × 1280 for the hero, is fine.
  • Width: at least three-quarters of the listed width, so the banner doesn't look blurry.
  • File size: at or under the KB limit. Compress the image if it's over.

Design tips

  • Banners are cropped to fill the space on some screens, so keep text away from the edges.
  • An AD label sits in the top-right corner. Leave that corner clear.
  • Use a few large words, not a paragraph. The slim banners are read in about a second.
  • Save photos as JPG or WebP, and flat graphics with text as PNG.
Sizes can change. MyPlug's admins can adjust ad spots. The Advertise page and your upload link always show the current size, so check them before you design.

How your photos are shown

  • Shop cards and the product gallery show photos square, cropped to fill. Put the item in the middle.
  • MyPlug may resize big photos (to about 1600 px wide) and convert them to WebP to keep pages fast. You don't need to do this yourself.
  • A photo over 4 MB is refused before any resizing happens. Shrink very large phone photos first.

Photos that sell

  • Daylight or bright, even light, with no flash glare.
  • A plain, tidy background, with the item filling most of the frame.
  • Your own photos of the actual item, especially for used goods.
  • No phone numbers or prices written on photos. Use the listing fields instead.
  • No other sellers' photos or watermarks.

4Free tools to make your files fit

You don't need expensive software. These free tools, most of which run in a web browser or on your phone, cover everything in this guide.

Design a banner

Canva
canva.com · web, phone
Choose Custom size and type the exact pixels, such as 1200 × 200. It has ready templates and exports JPG or PNG.
Adobe Express
adobe.com/express · web, phone
Banner templates, quick text effects and background removal.
Photopea
photopea.com · web
A free Photoshop-style editor in the browser. Use File → New at the exact size.
GIMP
gimp.org · Windows, Mac
Full desktop editor for detailed artwork.

Resize, crop and shrink

Squoosh
squoosh.app · web
Resize to exact pixels, convert to WebP or JPG and see the new KB size before you download. It's the best way to get under a banner's KB limit.
TinyPNG
tinypng.com · web
Drag in up to 20 images and get much smaller PNG, JPG or WebP files that look the same.
iLoveIMG
iloveimg.com · web
Crop to a square or exact size, and resize many photos at once.
Your phone
Photos / Gallery app
Edit → Crop → Square for listing photos. Snapseed (free) adds brightness and straightening.

Video, audio and PDF

HandBrake
handbrake.fr · Windows, Mac
Converts any video to MP4 using the Fast 1080p30 preset. Trim a trailer with the start and end times.
CapCut
capcut.com · web, phone
Cut a short trailer or preview clip and export MP4.
Audacity
audacityteam.org · Windows, Mac
Trim a preview clip and export MP3.
iLovePDF
ilovepdf.com · web
Word to PDF, compress PDF, and split out the first pages for a preview.

Check a file before you upload

Windows
Right-click the file → Properties → Details shows the width, height and size.
Mac
Open in Preview → Tools → Show Inspector, or select the file and press Cmd + I.
Phone
Open the photo and swipe up, or tap ⓘ, to see its resolution and file size.
Size check
1 MB = 1024 KB. A 400 KB banner limit means the file must show 400 KB or less.

6Before you upload

Quick checklist before you upload a banner

  • Exact size for the spot, or the same shape and larger
  • JPG, PNG or WebP
  • Under the KB limit (use Squoosh or TinyPNG)
  • Text inside the safe area
  • Top-right corner clear for the AD label
  • Link and headline double-checked

Quick checklist before you post a listing

  • JPG, PNG or WebP, 4 MB or less each
  • Square photos, item in the middle
  • Best photo first (it's the cover)
  • Real photos of the actual item
  • No phone numbers or prices on the photos
  • Digital: a preview of the same file type
These tools are made by other companies and aren't part of MyPlug. They're suggestions only. Check each one's terms before uploading private documents to it.
