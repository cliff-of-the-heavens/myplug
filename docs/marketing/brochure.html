Why MyPlug

Everything local, in one trusted place

MyPlug brings Malawi's shops, service providers, creators, property sellers, car dealers and couriers together, so you can find what you need and get it delivered safely.

Verified sellers

Registered businesses carry a green badge, checked by our team, and every seller has public ratings from real, delivered orders.

Safe handovers

Couriers are ID-checked. Every collection needs a pickup code or your secret phrase, and everyone is notified the moment it happens.

Your privacy

Sellers never see your email address. You choose which alerts you get, and can stop them any time.

Get started

Plug in today

  1. Visit www.myplug.mw, or install it on your phone from the browser menu (Add to home screen).
  2. Browse freely: no account needed to look around.
  3. Create an account to order, book, bid, chat and save favourites.
  4. Selling? Choose Sell on MyPlug. Once we approve your store, you're live.
