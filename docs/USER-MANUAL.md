# MyPlug User Manual

MyPlug is an online marketplace for Malawi: shops, service providers, digital creators, property sellers,
car dealers and couriers in one place. This manual explains how to use it for each type of account.

- [1. Getting started (everyone)](#1-getting-started-everyone)

## 1. Getting started (everyone)

### Accounts

| Account | For | How to get one |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer | Buying, booking, making offers, requests | **Create account** (top right) |
| Merchant | Selling products, services, downloads, property or vehicles | **Sell on MyPlug**; an admin approves the store before it goes live |
| Handyman | Tradespeople who repair and fix things (electronics, carpentry, metal work, mechanics, plumbing, wiring, gardening, cleaning) | **Join as a handyman** (footer), or **Create account → I fix things**; an admin checks your ID first |
| Courier | Delivering for stores and customers | **Create account → I deliver**; an admin checks your ID first |
| Administrator | Running the platform | Created by the site owner with `npm run setup` |

Anyone can browse without an account. You need to sign in to order, book, make an offer, chat or save
favourites.

### Signing in and out

- **Sign in** opens a pop-up. Enter your email and password, or tap **Continue with Google** / **Continue
  with Facebook** (when MyPlug has switched them on). If that account's email already has a MyPlug account,
  you're signed straight in. If you're new, you finish on the sign-up form (choose your account type, accept
  the terms) without making a password. You can add a password later with **Forgot password**.
- **Sign out**: open the account menu (your initial, top right) and choose **Sign out**.
- The account menu shows your email and account type.

### Forgot your password?

1. In the sign-in pop-up, choose **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email. If an account exists, MyPlug emails a reset link. It works **once, within one hour**.
3. Open the link, choose a new password (at least 8 characters). You're signed in, and signed out everywhere
   else.

### Change your password

Account menu → **Change password**. Enter your current password and the new one twice. Other devices are
signed out, and you get an email confirming the change. If you didn't make the change, reset your password
immediately.

### Terms of Use

You accept the Terms of Use when you sign up. When they change, you're asked to accept the new version on
your next visit.

### Notifications and messages

- The **bell** shows notifications (orders, offers, payments, reviews...). Important ones are also emailed.
- The **chat bubble** opens your messages with stores and customers.

### Finding things: the main menu

The coloured bar under the search box is the **main menu**:

**All products · Auto · Services · Handymen · Transport · Downloads · Real Estate**

Choose one and the browse page shows its **subcategories** as chips (e.g. under All products: Electronics,
Fashion...; under Handymen: the trades, such as Plumbing or Electronics repair). With nothing chosen, browsing shows **All products**. The search box searches everything.

## 2. Customers

### Browsing and searching

- Use the main menu and subcategory chips, or type in the search box.
- **Sort** by featured, price or newest; tick **In stock only** for products.
- **Auto:** choose **Vehicles** to filter by make, model, year, mileage, gearbox, fuel and body type, or
  **Spare parts** to filter by part category and find **parts that fit my vehicle** (make, model, year).
  You can also type a **part number** in the search box, with or without dashes.
- **Real Estate:** filter by type (land, house for sale, house to rent), **title deed available**, and key
  features (backup power, water tank, master en-suite, separate dining, borehole).

### Product, service and property pages

Each listing shows photos (tap thumbnails to see more), price, the seller, the seller's rating and
**Registered business** badge (if verified), ratings and reviews, and:

- **Products:** quantity, **Add to cart** or **Buy now**, or **Deliver on repeat** (e.g. daily bread).
- **Services:** an availability calendar and **Book**.
- **Downloads:** a free preview and price options (download, or watch/read online, sometimes time-limited).
- **Property:** see [Making an offer on property](#making-an-offer-on-property).
- **Vehicles and parts:** a full specification sheet (make, model, year, mileage, engine, gearbox,
  chassis number ending, duty status...) or the part number and the vehicles it fits.

Use the **heart** to save a listing, **Share** to send it to friends (WhatsApp, Facebook, X, email or a
link), **Ask a question** to chat with the seller, and **Report** if something is wrong or offensive.

### Favourites

Tap the heart on any product, or **Save store** on a store page. Find them all under account menu →
**Favourites**.

### Cart and checkout

1. Add items to the **cart** (cart icon, top right). Items are grouped by store; each store checks out
   separately.
2. At checkout choose or add a **delivery address**, and how the parcel is handed over: door, gate,
   reception, in person, or a **delegate** (name and phone).
3. Delivery depends on the store: the store delivers, the store's courier delivers, or **you arrange it**:
   collect yourself or send someone, ask up to 5 couriers for **quotes**, or use your usual courier.
4. **Pickup security** (when someone collects for you): MyPlug gives a **pickup code**, or you can set a
   **secret phrase**. You can also ask for an **extra check**: the store phones you, or you approve in the app.
5. **Payment:** *pay after delivery* (the bill is due 7 days after delivery) or *pay in advance*.
6. Enter a **promo code** or **gift coupon** code if you have one, then press **Apply** to see the new total.
7. Place the order.

### Your orders

Account menu → **My orders**. Each order shows tracking (placed → confirmed → shipped → delivered), the
delivery, and the bill.

- **Paying:** pay the store using the instructions shown, then **report your payment** with the
  transaction reference and/or a photo or PDF of the receipt. The store confirms it.
- **Repeat orders:** pause, resume or cancel under **Repeat orders**. Prepayments become credit that pays
  each delivery.

### Ratings and reviews

Once an order is **delivered**, the order page shows **Rate your order**. Give 1–5 stars and a comment for
each item, the seller and the courier. You can edit your rating later. Only your first name is shown. Rude
words are hidden automatically (e.g. "s***").

### Making an offer on property

On a property listing:

- **Negotiable:** enter the amount you're offering.
- **Non-negotiable:** press **I'm interested** to buy or rent at the asking price.
- **Bidding (reserve price):** bid at least the opening price and more than the current highest bid,
  before the closing date. You see the highest bid, the number of bidders and whether the secret reserve
  price has been met.

You can add a message, change your offer, or **Withdraw** it. The seller chooses the buyer; you're told
either way. Track everything under account menu → **Property offers**. **Never pay a deposit before viewing
the property and checking the documents** (including the title deed).

### Services and bookings

Pick a free date on the calendar and request the booking. The provider confirms it. Bookings show as
requested → confirmed → completed.

### Downloads (digital products)

Buy an option and report your payment. Once the seller confirms, open it from account menu → **My library**.
Time limits start then. Online-only options play in the browser.

### Image requests

Can't find something? **Requests → Post a request** with a photo and description. Merchants who can make or
source it message you.

### Rides, moving, parcels and car hire (Transport)

Open **Transport** in the main menu. The drivers and couriers there are registered and ID-checked by MyPlug.

- **Book a ride, a move or a parcel run:** choose what you need, enter pickup and drop-off, *as soon as
  possible* or a time, what's being moved, an optional budget and your phone number. Couriers who offer that
  service are told straight away and send you their price (the page updates by itself). **Accept** one:
  you then see their phone number and number plate, and they get yours. Pay them directly, as agreed.
  **Mark as done** afterwards, or **Cancel request**.
- **Hire a car:** browse **Cars for hire** (filter by seats or *with a driver*), open one, choose the first
  and last day and whether you want a driver, and **Request booking**. The total is worked out for you. The
  owner confirms or declines; once confirmed you see their phone number. Days that are already booked are
  listed on the car's page.
- Can't find the right car? **Post a car hire request** with your dates and owners will offer theirs.
- **Account menu → My transport** lists your requests and car bookings.
- **Rate the courier:** once a job is marked done, or a hire car is returned (or its last day has passed),
  give 1 to 5 stars and an optional comment. Ratings show next to couriers' offers and on their hire cars,
  so other customers can choose well.

### Hiring a handyman

- **Post a job:** on the **Handymen** tab tap **Post a job** (or choose a trade on **Post a request**). Say
  what needs doing and the area (not your full address), and add a photo if it helps. Every approved
  handyman in that trade is told straight away and messages you with a quote.
- **Browse profiles:** each handyman's profile shows their trades, where they work, photos of past work,
  references, an **ID verified** badge once MyPlug has checked their national ID, and community votes.
  Tap **Message** to ask a question, or book one of their listed services on a date.
- **Vote and comment:** anyone signed in can vote a handyman **up or down** once (vote again to change it),
  with an optional comment on the work. Accounts need to be a few days old to vote. Rude words are
  masked, and comments that break the rules are taken down.

### Email alerts

On a store or product page, tap **Get alerts** and choose *New arrivals*, *Back in stock* and/or
*Discounts*. Change or stop them under account menu → **Alerts**, or with the unsubscribe link in any email.
Stores never see your email address.

### Gift coupons

- **Buy:** on a store page, press **Gift coupon**, choose an amount, add the recipient's name and a message,
  and pay. Once the store confirms payment, you get a coupon page to share.
- **Spend:** anyone with the code can use it, online at checkout or in the shop. Whatever isn't used stays
  on the coupon. You're told every time it's used. Find yours under account menu → **Gift coupons**.

### Ad-free browsing

Account menu → **Ad-free browsing**. Pay for 1 month, 3 months (10% off) or 1 year (2 months free) to hide
banner strips, search banners and side banners. It starts once an admin confirms your payment.

## 3. Merchants (sellers)

### Opening your store

1. **Sell on MyPlug** and fill in your details, including your **business registration number**.
2. Under **Business info**, upload your registration certificate or business licence (the primary document)
   and any other licences, with numbers and dates.
3. Wait for an admin to approve the store. Until then your store is hidden.
4. Once your primary licence is verified, customers see a green **Registered business** badge. It
   disappears automatically if the licence expires; upload the renewed one.
5. **Verified merchant:** also give your **physical address** (shop, office or warehouse) under **Business
   info** and a phone number on your account. An admin checks your details and can then mark you as a
   **Verified merchant**: a green tick appears in the corner of all your listings and a "Verified merchant"
   badge on your store.

### Joining as a handyman

1. **Join as a handyman** (footer) or **Create account → I fix things**. Give your trading name (your own
   name is fine), phone, your **trades** and **where you work**. A business registration number is optional.
2. Under **ID & certificates**, upload your **national ID or passport** (the primary document). It's only
   for MyPlug's check and is never shown to customers. Add trade certificates if you have them.
3. Once an admin approves you, your profile goes live with a green **ID verified** badge.
4. **Profile**: add a photo, describe your work, update your trades and area, and list **references** (past
   clients customers can check). Add each service you offer as a listing (call-out fee, per job or per
   metre) with photos of past work; the photos also make up the **Past work** gallery on your profile.
5. **Jobs in my trades** lists open jobs customers posted in your trades; you're notified of each new one.
   Open a job and reply to quote.
6. **Reviews & votes** shows star reviews from bookings and the community's up/down votes. Reply publicly,
   or ask an admin to check a comment that's fake or abusive.
7. To get seen more, use **Advertise** (boost your listings on the Handymen tab, or book a banner) and pick a
   bigger **Store plan** if you need more listings.

### Your dashboard

**Seller → Overview** shows stock alerts, orders, unpaid bills, how many people saved your store, how many
times your store and listings were visited in the last 30 days, and your **store link** to share.

### Store settings

**Seller → Store**: name, description, logo, and **who delivers** (you, your chosen courier, or the
customer arranges it). Your store's currency is set by MyPlug (default: Malawian kwacha).

### Your store plan (how many listings)

**Seller → Store plan** shows your plan (e.g. *Starter, 25 listings, free*), how many you've used, and the
bigger plans. To upgrade, choose a plan and the number of months, pay using the reference shown, and report
your payment. Your plan changes once an admin confirms it. When a paid plan ends you go back to the free
plan: existing listings stay, but you can't add more beyond the free limit. Archived listings don't count.

### Adding listings

**Seller → Listings → New listing**, then choose what you're listing:

| Type | What to fill in |
| --- | --- |
| **A product** | Title, description, category, price (and a "was" price to show a deal), stock, photos |
| **A service** | Starting price, bookings you can take per day, references and past work, portfolio photos |
| **A digital product** | Kind of file, the full file, an optional free preview, and price options |
| **Real Estate** | See below |

**Photos:** one main photo plus extra photos (the number is set by MyPlug). Drag and drop several at once.

**Item location:** products are shown at your business address (from **Business info**) unless you choose
**Somewhere else** and list one or more places, e.g. two branches. Locations appear only on the item's own
page, with a map link, not in search results.

**Stock:** *I count my stock* (shows how many are left, sells out at 0) or *Always in stock* (continuous
supply). If you count stock, you can tick **Archive when sold out**: the listing is hidden at zero and
deleted after MyPlug's retention period, unless you restock it first.

**Vehicles and spare parts (category Auto):** choosing the **Auto** category adds the auto section:

- **A vehicle:** make, model, grade, year, mileage, engine size, gearbox, fuel, drive, steering, body type,
  colour, seats, doors, the last 6 characters of the chassis number (never the full number), condition,
  duty paid or not, in the country or being imported, location, and features (air conditioning, reverse
  camera, airbags...).
- **A spare part:** part category, part number, OEM number, brand, position, condition, and the list of
  **vehicles it fits** (make, model, years).

**Real Estate:**

- **Land / plot for sale:** a full description (the main information), location, plot size, title deed
  available or not. Photos are optional.
- **House for sale:** description, location, bedrooms and bathrooms, key features, title deed, and separate
  **exterior** and **interior** photos.
- **House to rent:** monthly rent, bedrooms and bathrooms, and the checklist: backup power, water tank,
  master en-suite, dining separate from the lounge, parking, wall/gate, borehole, furnished, staff
  quarters.
- **Price:** *Negotiable*, *Non-negotiable*, or *Reserve price (bidding)* with an opening bid, a secret
  reserve price and the date bidding ends. Once someone has bid, you can only extend the closing date.

You can send an **alert** to your followers when you save a listing (new arrival, back in stock, discount),
within MyPlug's weekly limit.

### Orders and bookings

**Seller → Orders & bookings.** Move each order along: confirm → processing → shipped → delivered (bookings:
confirm → completed). Mark **bills paid** when the money arrives. You can't dispatch a pay-in-advance order
until it's paid.

**Handing over to a collector:** the order page shows who is coming (name, phone, photo). Ask for the
**pickup code** or **secret phrase** and type in what you're told. If the customer asked for an extra
check, phone them or wait for their approval in the app. Everyone is notified when you hand over.

### Property offers

**Seller → Property offers** lists offers and bids on each property, best first, with the buyer's first
name and message. **Message** a buyer, **Decline** an offer, or **Accept** one: the property is marked
**Sold** (or **Let**), everyone else is told, and the listing is archived the next day. For bidding
listings you can accept only after bidding closes (you're notified when it does). If you sold it another
way, use **Sold elsewhere**.

### Reviews

**Seller → Reviews** shows what customers say about your store and products. Reply publicly, or **ask an
admin to check** a review that is abusive, false or from a competitor. The review stays up until an admin
decides.

### Deals and promo codes

**Seller → Deals & promo codes:**

- **Sales:** a percentage off everything or chosen items, with optional start and end times (presets: next
  24 hours, this weekend, end of month). Listings show the sale price and a countdown.
- **Promo codes:** a percentage or amount off at checkout, with optional minimum spend, dates, total uses and
  uses per customer.

### Gift coupons

**Seller → Gift coupons:** switch coupons on and set the smallest and largest amounts and how long they
last. Mark coupon orders paid to activate them. To take a coupon **in the shop**, enter the code under
**Take a coupon at the counter**, check the balance and take the amount.

### Repeat orders

**Seller → Repeat orders** lists customers' standing orders (e.g. daily bread). Each day's delivery appears
as a normal order.

### Advertising

- **Product boosts** (**Seller → Advertise**): Boost, Featured or Premium placements put your products
  higher in the storefront, search and Sponsored picks, shared fairly between stores.
- **Banner ads** (**Advertise on MyPlug**, link in the footer): the home page hero, the home banner strip,
  the search banner, or the side banner. The page lists each spot's exact size, file size limit and weekly
  price, and how to prepare your banner. Upload it, an admin confirms the price, you pay, and the admin
  schedules it. Your banner links to your store unless you give another link. You can see its views, time
  on screen and clicks.

## 4. Couriers

1. **Create account → I deliver.**
2. Under **Profile & prices**, upload a clear **face photo** and your **ID document**, enter your national ID
   number, vehicle and plate, and your **estimated prices per area or city**.
3. Wait for an admin to compare your photo with your ID and approve you.
4. Your **Courier dashboard** lists delivery requests. Confirm the real price (or send a quote when a
   customer asks several couriers). The customer accepts one quote, which closes the others.
5. Each job shows the store, pickup address and phone, pickup time, items, destination and handover
   instructions. At the store, give the **pickup code**.
6. On arrival, mark the order **delivered**, noting who received it or where you left it.
7. **My ratings** shows customers' ratings, for store deliveries, transport jobs and car hire. You can ask an
   admin to check an unfair review.

### Rides, moving, parcels and car hire

8. On your **Profile**, choose your **vehicle type** and **capacity**, and tick what else you offer:
   **Taxi**, **Moving**, **Parcel** and **Car hire**.
9. **Transport jobs** lists customers' open requests for those services (you're notified of each new one).
   Open one and **send your price** with a short note (vehicle, arrival time). You can change or withdraw
   it while the request is open. If the customer accepts, you get their phone number; **Mark as done** when
   finished.
10. **Hire cars:** add each car with photos, seats, gearbox, price per day, where it's collected, and whether
    it's self-drive, with a driver (and the extra per day), or both. Booking requests appear at the top:
    **Confirm** or **Decline** (with a reason). You can't confirm two bookings on the same days. Press **Car
    returned** at the end.

## 5. Administrators

Sign in with the admin account and open **Admin** (account menu). Numbers beside menu items show what's
waiting for you.

### Overview and analytics

- **Overview:** counts of everything waiting.
- **Analytics:** page views and visitors per day, the most visited stores and listings, banner ad
  performance (views, average time on screen, clicks, click rate), the signed-in customers who visit stores
  most this month and which stores they frequent, and a monthly summary. Choose 7, 30 or 90 days.
  **Settings and retention** switches recording on or off and sets how long day-by-day detail,
  who-visits-which-store and monthly totals are kept. Recording happens after pages load and never slows
  the site.

### Merchants, couriers and customers

- **Merchants:** approve or reject applications (a reason is required), suspend and reactivate, set each
  store's **currency** and **plan**, open business documents, **verify** or reject them, and choose which
  documents customers may see.
- **Verified merchant:** each merchant shows a checklist (primary document verified and in date,
  registration number, physical address, phone). Once the document is verified you can **Mark as
  verified**, with a note of what you checked (e.g. visited the shop). The merchant is told, and their
  listings get a green tick. **Remove verification** takes it away (give a reason; they're told).
- **Handymen** appear among merchants with a **Handyman** tag, their trades and area. Verify their national
  ID (their primary document) before approving; never mark an ID as shown to customers.
- **Couriers:** compare the face photo with the ID document, then approve, reject or suspend.
- **Customers:** search, see orders and unpaid bills, suspend and reactivate. **Details** shows everything
  about one account.

### Listing plans

**Admin → Listing plans:** create and edit plans (name, items allowed, monthly price). Exactly one free
plan is the default for every store. Approve or reject **plan requests** after checking payment. Set
**photos per listing** (1–30).

### Product boosts and payment instructions

- **Product boosts:** quote custom requests, check payments, approve boosts, stop them early.
- **Boost bands & payment:** boost prices, and the **payment instructions** (bank / mobile money) shown on
  every invoice in MyPlug.

### Banner ads

**Admin → Banner ads:**

- **Needs action:** member requests (check the banner, confirm or change the price, which asks the
  advertiser to pay), payments to check, and approval with a start date. Each spot rotates a limited number
  of ads at once; approval is refused when the spot is full, and the message says when a slot frees up.
- **New ad for a non-member:** for someone who emailed or phoned. Upload their banner yourself, or send a
  **one-time upload link** with a **6-digit passcode**. The passcode is shown once; give it by phone or SMS,
  or include it in the email. The link expires after 14 days and locks after 5 wrong passcodes. Record the
  payment reference and approve.
- **Ad-free passes:** confirm payments to switch ad-free browsing on.
- **Spots, prices & hero panel:** each spot's size, file limit, weekly price, how many ads rotate at once,
  whether it's on sale, and whether ad-free pass holders see it; what appears beside the home hero
  (*Top 5 selling shops this week*, *Top 5 rated shops*, or nothing); the email address for ad enquiries;
  and the ad-free price.

### Email alerts

**Admin → Email alerts:** switch alerts off for everyone, set alerts per store per week, the wait before
repeating an alert, and the minimum discount for discount alerts; pause a store's alerts; preview sent
emails.

### Transport

**Admin → Transport** shows the last 30 days of rides, moves, parcel runs and car hire. The **Transport**
menu badge counts requests that have waited over 2 hours without a single offer; tap the yellow card to see
them.

- **Requests:** filter by type and status, or search by reference (TR-…), customer or courier. Open a request
  to see the details, every offer, both phone numbers and the customer's rating. **Cancel request** (with a
  reason) for fake, abusive or duplicate requests; the customer and any booked courier are told why.
- **Car hire:** **Hide car** (with a reason sent to the owner) for misleading photos, wrong prices or reported
  cars, and **Show again** later. **Cancel booking** (with a reason) tells both the customer and the owner.
- To suspend a courier, use **Couriers**; courier names here link there.

### Moderation and reviews

- **Moderation:** reported images, listings and stores. Remove, restore, dismiss, or suspend the owner.
  Images reported by several people are hidden automatically until you decide.
- **Reviews:** flagged reviews first. **Take down**, **Keep it up** or **Put back up**; everyone involved is
  told. The **Handyman comments** tab does the same for community votes on handymen; a taken-down
  comment's vote stops counting. Edit the **obscene-word filter**: add local-language words to block, and words to allow.

### System settings

- **File storage:** Vercel Blob, or your own **FTP/FTPS** server on another domain (host, port,
  encryption, username, password, folder, and the public web address the folder is served from). Use
  **Test connection** before saving.
- **Image optimisation:** when on, uploaded photos are resized (maximum width) and converted to WebP at the
  quality you choose. This applies to photos uploaded from then on.
- **Sold and sold-out listings:** how many days archived property and sold-out products are kept before
  they're deleted.
- **Inactive stores:** archive after N months, warn, delete after N months.
- **Run the daily jobs now:** runs everything the nightly job does.

## 6. Safety tips for everyone

- MyPlug connects buyers and sellers; it doesn't hold money. Pay only through the methods the seller or
  MyPlug shows, and keep your transaction reference.
- Check the **Registered business** badge, ratings and reviews before big purchases.
- For property and vehicles, **view in person and check documents** (title deed, registration, duty papers,
  chassis number) before paying anything.
- Never share your password, pickup code or secret phrase except at handover.
- Report anything suspicious with the **Report** button.
